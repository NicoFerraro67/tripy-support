Tripy support

Need help with your trips, photos or places? Email nicoaferraro@gmail.com.

Include your iOS version, Tripy version and what happened. Please avoid sending private photos or detailed travel history unless they are needed to investigate your request.

Your photos stay yours

Removing a trip or photo from Tripy leaves the original in Apple Photos unchanged. Imported photos may need to download from iCloud Photos before they can open.

Local storage

The current app saves its journal on your device and has no dedicated cross-device journal sync. Keep device backups enabled. Removing the app can remove its local journal; restoring Pro purchases does not restore trips.
