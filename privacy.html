Privacy policy

Updated November 8, 2026

Your privacy

Tripy helps you keep a journal of your trips, photos, videos and saved places. This policy explains how the current iPhone app handles that information.

Your journal and media

Your trip names, dates, notes, destinations and saved places are stored on your device. Tripy does not operate a server that receives your journal or hosts your photos and videos.

When you grant Photos access, Tripy can use the dates and locations attached to permitted photos and videos to suggest trips. You review what to import. Imported media generally remains in your Photos library; Tripy saves references and may generate local preview images. Photos or videos added through the system photo picker may be copied into Tripy's local storage. Media stored in iCloud Photos may be downloaded by Apple's services when you view it.

Trip suggestions can be wrong, including when you save photos someone else sent you. A photo location does not prove that you visited a business. Tripy does not continuously track your device's location or receive your historical home address from Apple.

Maps and external services

Tripy uses Apple Maps and Apple's place search and geocoding services. Map requests, searches and coordinates used for these services are handled by Apple under its policies. Opening a destination in Apple Maps or opening a source/license link takes you to that service. Tripy does not sell your journal, use advertising trackers or include an advertising service in the current app.

Country flags and generalized country/region maps are bundled with the app. Their sources and licenses are available from the map credit. Land coverage represents the estimated land area of recorded regions, not ground you physically explored.

Your choices and retention

You can use manual trip entry without granting access to your whole Photos library. You control Photos permissions, including limited access, in iPhone Settings. Changing permissions can make previously linked media unavailable.

Removing a photo from a trip, removing a trip/place, or resetting Tripy does not remove the original from Apple Photos. Reset App removes the local journal and app-managed selected-media copies. Temporary previews may remain until the operating system or app clears its cache. Removing the app removes its local app container; device backups are governed by your Apple settings. Tripy currently has no dedicated cross-device journal sync or account backup service.

Support and updates

If you contact support, the information you choose to send is used to respond to your request. Do not send sensitive trip details or media unless needed for your request. Contact: nicoaferraro@gmail.com. Material changes to how Tripy handles information will be reflected in this policy.
