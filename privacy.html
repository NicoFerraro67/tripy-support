Privacy policy

Updated November 13, 2026

Your privacy

Tripy helps you keep a journal of your trips, photos, videos and saved places. This policy explains how the current iPhone app handles that information.

Your journal and media

Your trip names, dates, notes, destinations and saved places are stored on your device. Tripy does not operate a server that receives your journal or hosts your photos and videos.

When you grant Photos access, Tripy can use the dates and locations attached to permitted photos and videos to suggest trips. You review what to import. Imported media generally remains in your Photos library; Tripy saves references and may generate local preview images. Photos or videos added through the system photo picker may be copied into Tripy's local storage. Media stored in iCloud Photos may be downloaded by Apple's services when you view it.

Postcards are created on your device from the trip photos and text you choose. Tripy does not upload them to a server. Choosing Share sends the generated image to the app or recipient you select, under that service's policies. Choosing Save image requests permission to add the postcard to Photos. Finished postcards are saved in Tripy’s local Postcards library so you can reopen, share or save them later. Temporary export files are cleared after the local copy is saved.

Trip suggestions can be wrong, including when you save photos someone else sent you. A photo location does not prove that you visited a business. Tripy does not continuously track your device's location or receive your historical home address from Apple.

Maps and external services

Tripy uses Apple Maps and Apple's place search and geocoding services. Map requests, searches and coordinates used for these services are handled by Apple under its policies. Opening a destination in Apple Maps or opening a source/license link takes you to that service. Tripy does not sell your journal, use advertising trackers or include an advertising service in the current app.

Country flags and generalized country/region maps are bundled with the app. Boundary sources and licenses are available from the map credit. Flat flag artwork comes from the MIT-licensed flag-icons project. Land coverage represents the estimated land area of recorded regions, not ground you physically explored.

Optional usage analytics

Share usage analytics is optional and off until you choose it. Our first-party service receives per-install action counts, operation durations, coarse error categories, purchase outcomes, library counts and country-level recorded-trip totals. A random installation identifier connects these events; this is pseudonymous usage, not fully anonymous data.

No journal text, trip names, travel dates, photos, videos, exact locations, addresses, names, recipients, advertising identifiers or raw transaction IDs are included. No advertising tracking, cross-app identity or session replay is used. Your journal and media stay local.

Raw activity and last-observed count snapshots are retained for up to 90 days. Installation records expire after 90 days of inactivity. Menu → Usage analytics lets you stop collection and erase that installation’s analytics. Opt-out clears queued activity immediately; when offline, deletion is retained and retried before any future uploads. Your trips are not changed. Erasing the local journal also requests analytics erasure. A keyed one-way digest of an erased installation identifier may be retained for up to 90 days only to prevent delayed requests from recreating erased analytics. It contains no journal content or readable installation identifier.

Purchases

Tripy Pro is an optional one-time purchase processed by Apple. Apple handles billing and payment details. Tripy checks Apple's signed purchase records on your device to unlock features and restore eligible purchases. Tripy does not receive your payment card details or require a Tripy account.

Your Apple Account purchase can be restored independently of your local trip journal. Restoring a purchase does not restore journal data; that depends on your device backup or transfer and access to the corresponding Photos library.

Your choices and retention

You can use manual trip entry without granting access to your whole Photos library. You control Photos permissions, including limited access, in iPhone Settings. Changing permissions can make previously linked media unavailable.

Removing a photo from a trip or removing a trip/place does not remove the original from Apple Photos. Photos attached to a place also appear in its trip gallery. Temporary previews may remain until the operating system or app clears its cache. Removing the app removes its local app container; device backups are governed by your Apple settings. Tripy currently has no dedicated cross-device journal sync or account backup service.

Support and updates

If you contact support, the information you choose to send is used to respond to your request. Do not send sensitive trip details or media unless needed for your request. Contact: nicoaferraro@gmail.com. Material changes to how Tripy handles information will be reflected in this policy.
